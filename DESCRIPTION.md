Marram is a free theme for stores that want to say less and show more.

It is minimal and airy, and it fits any kind of store: a pale fog-gray ground, light, open headings, hairline rules and wide margins, with every product set on its own quiet tile. It works as well for a handful of products as for a few hundred.

**Words first, then the work.** The home page opens on a large, light heading with one short line and one link set apart beside it. Add a photo when you have one, as a wide band under the words or a tall picture beside them. Until then the page stays a calm band of type and space, never a placeholder.

**Products with room to breathe.** Each photo sits on a pale tile with a wide margin of air, the name and price on one quiet line underneath. Choose two, three or four tiles per row, an inset or edge-to-edge photo, and a heading above the tiles or beside them. A second photo on hover, color dots and an add-to-cart button are switches in the theme settings.

**One product in focus.** Give a single product a whole band of the page: its photo large, with its name, price, a line from its description and a link to its page.

**Your categories as an index.** Categories become a numbered list of large names on hairline rows, each with a small photo and an arrow that moves on hover. The same index lays out the categories page.

**A spare product page.** A slim column of photo links, the chosen photo large in the middle, and a narrow column of details that stays in view: options, subscriptions and trials, the product's up-sells, its description, a ruled table of its attributes, and your own notes on shipping, returns and care.

**A few words, set large.** A statement section sets one sentence about your store with room all around it, and text columns hold up to four short notes side by side. About and Contact page layouts come ready to fill.

**Every page, built in.** Like every Wave theme, Marram lays out every Swell page type: home, category, product, search, cart, account, blog, content pages, policies and more. The cart opens in a drawer, and customers can track orders, buy again and manage subscriptions. The header and footer are shared by every page, and every fixed word shoppers see is yours to reword in Store text.

**Edit it in Wave.** Click any section or line of text to change it, and move, hide or add sections. Set fonts, type size, spacing, page width, buttons and cards for the whole store in the Theme tab. Or ask Wave's AI agent in plain words; when Marram has no section for what you want, it writes one in its style. Grids, categories, menus and the blog read your Swell data, so new products appear on their own.

**Free.** Marram costs nothing to install, customize or publish.

**Good to know.** Marram reads your products, categories, menus, contact details and policies from Swell, so set those up first. Sections that need your words or picks (the statement, text columns and the product in focus) stay hidden until you fill them. There is no photo zoom, quick view, sticky add-to-cart bar or mega menu, and filters list options as text. Star ratings appear only with a reviews app installed.
